# 🦅 CekRego — Brand & Logo Guidelines

## 1. The Mark: Radar Falcon Shield
The **CekRego** mark combines a chamfered hexagonal **Security Shield** (representing on-chain contract auditing & RugCheck protection) with a negative-space **Falcon Profile** and **Cyber Cyan Hunter Eye** (representing high-speed DexScreener & Pump.fun on-chain radar).

- **Construction Grid:** `256 × 256` integer canvas, `45°` & `90°` geometric angles, `fill-rule="evenodd"`.
- **Small-Size Cut:** `cekrego-symbol-small.svg` is optically simplified for `16px–24px` favicons.

## 4. Clear Space & Minimum Size
- **Clear Space:** Maintain at least `12.5%` (`32 units` on the `256` grid) of clear space around the shield on all sides.
- **Minimum Size:**
  - Standard symbol: `24px` height.
  - Small-size cut (`cekrego-symbol-small.svg`): `16px` height.
